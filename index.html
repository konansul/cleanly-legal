Cleanly

Photo and storage cleanup for iPhone

Cleanly finds what is quietly filling your phone: the same shot saved twice, bursts you never sorted, screenshots you read once, out-of-focus frames and the heaviest videos. Swipe sorts everything else one photo at a time, left to the trash and right to keep. It also measures folders you point it at, so PDFs, archives and forgotten downloads are counted too, and finds contacts saved twice.

Photos, files and contacts are read on the device and never uploaded, and there is no account. Photos are compared with Apple's Vision framework on your iPhone. The app counts anonymous usage and sends anonymous crash reports; no file name, photo or contact is ever part of that.
