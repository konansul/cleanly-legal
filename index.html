Cleaner

Photo and storage cleanup for iPhone

Cleaner finds what is quietly filling your phone: the same shot saved twice, bursts you never sorted, screenshots you read once, out-of-focus frames and the heaviest videos. It also measures folders you point it at, so PDFs, archives and forgotten downloads are counted too.

Everything runs on the device. There is no account, no server and no analytics. Photos are compared using Apple's Vision framework on your iPhone, and nothing is ever uploaded.
