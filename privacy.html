Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Cleanly ("the app", "we", "us"), published on the App Store as Cleanly: Photo Cleaner, finds duplicate photos, near-identical shots, blurred frames, screenshots, heavy videos and oversized files, and helps you remove them. It runs entirely on your device. This policy explains what happens to your data, which in this case is short: it never leaves your phone. If you have questions, contact us at konansulx@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

None. We do not collect, transmit, store or receive any information about you, your photos or your files. The app has no server of any kind, and we have no way to see what is in your library.

  • No account, no sign-in. There is nothing to register. The app never asks for your name, email address, phone number or password.
  • No analytics, no tracking. The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifier and no tracking of any kind.
  • No network requests. The app makes no internet connections of its own. Switch the network off and every feature still works.

2. Data that stays on your device

  • Your photos and videos. The app reads your photo library to measure and compare it. Comparison uses Apple's Vision framework on the device. Images are never uploaded.
  • Photo library access. iOS asks for your permission before the app can see your library. You can grant access to all photos or to a selection, and you can change or revoke it at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Analysis cache. To avoid rescanning from scratch, the app stores image fingerprints and scan results in its own private storage on the device. This cache contains no images, only numeric signatures. Deleting the app removes it.
  • Folders you choose. The Files tab measures only a folder you pick yourself through the system file picker. iOS grants the app access to that folder alone. Nothing else on the device is readable, and the folder contents are never uploaded.
  • Your contacts. The Contacts tab reads your address book to find the same person saved more than once, one number stored under two names, and cards with no way to reach anyone. Names, numbers and addresses are compared on the device and are never uploaded, and the app keeps no copy of them after you leave the tab.
  • Contacts access. iOS asks for your permission before the app can read your address book, and the Contacts tab is the only part of the app that uses it. You can revoke it at any time in iOS Settings, and the rest of the app carries on working without it.
  • iCloud files. If a file you open is stored only in iCloud, the app asks iOS to download it so it can be previewed. This request goes from your device to Apple, not to us. The app never downloads files on its own.

3. Deleting things

Nothing is deleted without you choosing it and confirming. Photos and videos are removed through the system, which places them in Recently Deleted, where iOS keeps them for 30 days before they are gone for good. Files removed from a folder you selected, and contacts removed from the Contacts tab, are deleted immediately and cannot be recovered from within the app.

4. Purchases

Cleanly offers an optional Pro upgrade as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription or a one-time purchase. Payments are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app asks Apple whether a purchase is active and stores nothing about you.

5. Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.
